HOW THE SYSTEM WORKS

One record follows the material from request to audit.

  1. 01

    Login & authenticate

    Role-based access validates Admin and Staff sessions.

  2. 02

    Manage inventory

    Store tools, material details, quantities, and locations.

  3. 03

    Issue or return

    Record the recipient, section, quantity, and time of every handover.

  4. 04

    Review & report

    Retrieve an immutable transaction history for audit and reporting.
